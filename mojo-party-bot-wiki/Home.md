## What is Mojo Party Bot

A Discord bot for finding groups (LFG), built to work identically across very different activities: competitive shooters, MMOs, tabletop RPGs, and simulation games. See the [README](https://github.com/mojouto3/mojo-party-bot#readme) for a technical overview.
